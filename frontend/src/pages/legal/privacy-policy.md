## 1. Introduction

Clinic AI Assistant ("Clinic AI Assistant," "the Platform," "we," "us," or "our") is a digital healthcare management platform designed to support appointment scheduling, patient record management, healthcare provider coordination, medical document organization, and AI-assisted information services.

We recognize that information concerning an individual's health is highly sensitive and deserves careful protection.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal and health-related information we collect, why we process it, how it may be shared, the safeguards applied to it, and the choices and rights available to individuals.

We seek to handle health information in accordance with applicable privacy and healthcare laws and recognized principles of confidentiality, data minimization, accountability, and information security.

This Policy applies to visitors and registered users, including patients, doctors, receptionists, and administrators, except where a separate healthcare provider privacy notice or legally required agreement governs particular information.

## 2. Our Role in Processing Health Information

Depending on how the Platform is used, we may process information on our own behalf or provide technology services to participating healthcare organizations.

Where a clinic or healthcare organization determines the purposes and means of processing patient records, that organization may be responsible for decisions concerning those records, and Clinic AI Assistant may process the information under its instructions and applicable contractual obligations.

Where United States HIPAA requirements apply, the respective responsibilities of covered entities, business associates, and subcontractors are determined by applicable law and agreements.
